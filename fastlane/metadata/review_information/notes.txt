Rulebook manages the server-side mail rules on a Microsoft 365 or Outlook.com
mailbox. It needs a signed-in Microsoft account to show anything, so demo
credentials are supplied in the fields above.

Sign-in is presented by Microsoft (MSAL), not by Rulebook. If Microsoft's page
offers a passkey or security key first, go Back and choose another way to
sign in, using the password. After signing in, the rules list is the first
screen; tap any rule for its detail.

Viewing rules is free. Changing them needs Rulebook Pro, a one-time,
non-consumable in-app purchase (net.steinbok.Rulebook.pro): creating, editing,
deleting, reordering, and turning rules on or off. Those controls show a lock
and open the purchase screen, which also has Restore purchase and Redeem
code. Buy Pro with a sandbox account to reach the editor (Edit on a rule's
detail screen).

Microsoft Graph scopes requested, and why:
 - MailboxSettings.ReadWrite: read and write the mail rules themselves
 - Mail.ReadBasic: list mail folders, so rules can name and pick them;
   the app reads no message data
 - offline_access: stay signed in between launches

The app never requests permission to read message bodies. The privacy policy
at https://rulebook.steinbok.net/privacy.html discloses that the folder-listing
permission is broader than the app's use of it.
